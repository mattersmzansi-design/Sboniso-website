Our story

Development that goes beyond bricks and mortar.

Squnga’sihle Trading (Pty) Ltd was founded with a singular and purposeful mandate — to accelerate the capacity and market entry of emerging Implementing Agent companies in South Africa’s critical service delivery ecosystem. We are purpose-built for impact: equipped to deliver end-to-end solutions across the urban and rural housing sector while extending our footprint into strategic engineering infrastructure.

Every project we touch represents a community served, a family empowered, and a municipality transformed.

Leadership that delivers

Mr. S.W. Cele — CEO

Our CEO, Mr. Siboniso Wiseman Cele, brings over five years of hands-on experience in KwaZulu-Natal’s housing development sector. A graduate of the University of Zululand with a BSc in Geography and Hydrology (2016), and a certified practitioner in Fundamentals of Project Management (2019), he combines academic depth with practical mastery across project management, social facilitation and community engagement.
