Who We Are

End-to-end delivery for the housing sector

Squnga’esihle Trading (Pty) Ltd is a project management, social facilitation and housing development enterprise built for impact. We understand that true development goes beyond bricks and mortar; it is about people, communities, and the futures we collectively build. Every project we touch represents a community served, a family empowered, and a municipality transformed.

What We Do

Core Service Offerings

Project Management
  • Project start-up meetings with clients
  • Procurement & management of service providers
  • Project kick-off & technical meetings
  • Tranche application packaging to DOHS
  • Representation at housing forum meetings
  • Monthly reporting to DOHS, LM & PSC
Social Facilitation
  • Community engagement & stakeholder mapping
  • Formation of Community Based Partners
  • Electing Project Steering Committees
  • Socio-Economic Report compilation
  • CBP constitution & Social Compact Agreement
  • Co-ordination of community meetings & minutes
Subsidy & Sales Admin
  • Beneficiary list compilation
  • Completion of subsidy administration forms
  • Submission to DOHS for approval
  • GPS coordinates for each approved beneficiary
  • P2 GPS survey certification per point
  • SPLUMA submissions & site verification
